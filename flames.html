Twin Flames

  • Twin Flames: Web Build

Tools: Unity, C#

This was a solo project. It is a fantasy/sci-fi game with a core mirror mechanic. Players must use the mirror's special abilities to advance to the next round.

The mirror can pass through certain platforms...

...It can also fall through them!

Players also have the ability to swap their sprites to beat the level.
